Hospitality sustainability tool

Turn food waste data into smarter decisions.

A simple interactive prototype to help cafés see what is wasted, estimate its cost and review practical next steps.

1Input business data
2Analyse waste patterns
3Get recommendations
4Review actions

Step 1. Enter café waste data

Enter one week's figures for each food item. Or load illustrative sample data for the live demo.

How to use: Enter the quantity prepared or purchased, the quantity wasted, and the approximate cost per unit (£). For example, for a pastry use individual pastries; for milk use litres. The figures and outputs are illustrative, not actual customer results.

How to measure progress

Compare results week by week, alongside customer demand and product availability.

  • Quantity of food wasted per week, by item and unit
  • Estimated financial value of wasted products
  • Amount of spoiled ingredients
  • Food waste per customer (if customer counts are available)
  • Estimated cost savings over time, once new data is collected
